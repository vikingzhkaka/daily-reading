Why Young Chinese Are Falling in Love with Second-Hand Shopping

In recent years, a quiet but powerful change has taken place in the way young Chinese people shop. Instead of buying everything brand new, a growing number of consumers in their twenties and thirties are turning to the second-hand market. On popular platforms such as Xianyu, millions of used phones, clothes, books, and furniture are sold every day, and the total value of this trade has risen faster than many expected.

What explains this sudden warmth toward things that someone else has owned? The first reason is practical. As living costs in big cities climb, young buyers have become more careful with their money. A second-hand item often costs half as much as a new one, yet it can work just as well. Beyond price, environmental awareness plays a part. Many young people now believe that reusing a product is a simple way to reduce waste and protect the planet. Because they see value in sustainability, they feel good about their choices.

The effect on daily life has been noticeable. For some, shopping second-hand has become a weekend habit, almost like visiting a market with friends. Online communities have formed around the hobby, where users share photos of their finds and give tips on what to avoid. This social side has made the practice feel less like a compromise and more like a smart lifestyle.

However, the model is not perfect. Since anyone can post an item, buyers sometimes meet dishonest sellers or receive goods that differ from the description. To keep the market healthy, platforms must build stronger check systems and protect users who are treated unfairly.

In the end, the rise of second-hand shopping reflects a broader shift in values. Younger generations are learning that owning less can mean living better, and that a product's worth lies not in whether it is new, but in whether it is useful.

中文译文

近年来，中国年轻人购物的方式发生了一场悄然却有力的变化。越来越多的二三十岁消费者不再样样都买全新的，而是转向二手市场。在闲鱼等热门平台上，每天都有数以百万计的二手手机、衣物、书籍和家具成交，而这类交易的总价值增长之快超出了许多人的预期。

是什么让人们对别人用过的东西突然有了热情？首要原因是实用。随着大城市生活成本攀升，年轻买家在花钱上变得更加谨慎。一件二手物品的价格往往只有新品的一半，却同样好用。除了价格，环保意识也在起作用。如今许多年轻人相信，重复使用一件商品是减少浪费、保护地球的一种简单方式。因为他们看到了可持续的价值，所以对自己的选择感到安心。

这对日常生活的影响已经显现。对一些人来说，买二手货成了一种周末习惯，几乎像和朋友逛集市一样。围绕这一爱好还形成了线上社群，用户在里面分享淘到的好物，并给出避坑建议。这种社交属性让这件事不再像是一种将就，而更像一种聪明的生活方式。

不过，这种模式并非完美。由于任何人都能发布商品，买家有时会遇到不诚实的卖家，或收到与描述不符的货物。为了让市场保持健康，平台必须建立更严格的审核机制，并保护受到不公对待的用户。

归根结底，二手购物的兴起折射出价值观的一场更广泛转变。年轻一代正在认识到：拥有更少，或许意味着生活得更好；一件商品的价值不在于它是否崭新，而在于它是否有用。
