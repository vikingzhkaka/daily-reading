翻译练习 · 太极拳（Tai Chi）

原题（中文）

太极拳（Tai Chi）是中国的一种传统武术，起源于中国古代，至今已有数百年的历史。它动作缓慢柔和，注重身心平衡，深受各年龄段人群的喜爱。如今，太极拳不仅在中国广受欢迎，也在世界许多地方得到推广。

你的译文

Tai Chi is a Chinese traditional martial art. It originated from ancient China and has hundreds years history. Its movements are slow and soft, it focuses on balance of body and mind, is loved by people of all ages. Today, Tai Chi not only popular in China, but also promoted in many places of the world.

批改

主要问题

  1. 数词搭配：hundreds years history → a history of several hundred years（或 hundreds of years of history）。
  2. 连写句（run-on）：Its movements are slow and soft, it focuses on... 两个独立分句仅用逗号连接，应改为从句或分词结构，例如 Characterized by slow and gentle movements, it emphasizes...。
  3. 形容词搭配：slow and soft → slow and gentle（描述武术/动作更常用 gentle）。
  4. 缺系动词：Tai Chi not only popular in China 缺少 is，应为 is not only popular in China。
  5. 介词短语：many places of the world → many parts of the world。

亮点

  • 整体意思传达准确，时态与基本结构清晰，专有名词 Tai Chi 使用正确。

参考译文

Tai Chi is a traditional form of Chinese martial arts that originated in ancient China and boasts a history of several hundred years. Characterized by slow and gentle movements, it emphasizes the balance between body and mind and is deeply loved by people of all age groups. Today, Tai Chi is not only widely popular in China but has also been introduced to many parts of the world.
