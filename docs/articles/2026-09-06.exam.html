The Teacher Who Walked Every Morning

In a mountain village where the road was poor and the winters long, a young teacher named Lin decided that she would not leave. She had grown up there, and she believed that children who stayed behind deserved the same chance as those in the city. When the old school lost its only teacher, the classes stopped, and many parents began to send their children away to work. Lin saw this, and she felt that something had to change.

She started with a single room and a few desks, teaching reading and maths before the sun was high. At first, only six children came, yet she kept going, certain that trust grows slowly. Neighbours who had once doubted her began to help, bringing food, books, and warm clothes. Because the village was remote, she walked two hours each morning to reach the nearest town for supplies. Although the heating failed in winter, she never cancelled a lesson, for she knew that a missed day might cause a child to lose heart.

Within three years, the small class had grown to thirty, and the children's scores rose above the county average. A report published by a local paper described her work, and the story brought quiet recognition from readers across the region. What mattered most, however, was not the praise but the fact that fewer children left. Lin's example shows that a teacher's steady presence can reshape a community more than any large plan. Schools elsewhere now study her method, turning one quiet effort into a model for the rural countryside.

中文译文

在一个山路难行、冬日漫长的山村，一位名叫林的年轻教师决定不离开。她在那里长大，相信留下来的孩子理应和城市里的孩子拥有同样的机会。当旧学校失去唯一的老师，课就停了，许多家长开始把孩子送去外地打工。林看在眼里，觉得事情必须改变。

她从一间空房、几张课桌起步，在太阳升高前教孩子们读写和算术。起初只来了六个孩子，她却坚持下来，坚信信任是慢慢长出来的。曾经怀疑她的邻居开始伸出援手，送来食物、书本和冬衣。因为村庄偏远，她每天清晨要走两小时到最近的镇上采购物资。尽管冬天暖气坏了，她从不停课，因为她知道缺一天课，孩子也许就会灰心。

三年之内，这个小班的规模增至三十人，孩子们的成绩超过了全县平均线。一家地方报纸发表的报道讲述了她的事迹，这个故事为她带来了全区域读者安静的认可。然而最重要的不是赞扬，而是离开的孩子变少了。林的例子说明，一位教师稳定的陪伴比任何宏大计划都更能重塑一个社区。别处的学校如今研究她的方法，把一个安静的努力变成乡村的范本。
