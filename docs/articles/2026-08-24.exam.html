Why Your Brain Needs Sleep

Modern life has quietly shortened our nights. Surveys show that many adults now sleep fewer than seven hours, and a surprising number of them feel sleepy long before evening. This shift was not an accident; it grew out of brighter screens, longer working hours, and the quiet habit of reading phones in the dark. Coffee hides the debt for a while, yet it cannot remove what the night failed to repair. Teenagers, whose bodies still demand growth, need even more rest, yet school starts early and leaves them short.

What does lost sleep actually cost us? Scientists explain that the brain washes away waste products while we rest, which is why poor sleep blurs memory and slows the mind. When people miss rest for several weeks, their mood drops and their body resists illness less effectively. Children who study late face the same problem, and their grades often fall before anyone notices the cause. It is not only the brain that suffers — the heart, too, strains without recovery, and the risk of weight gain rises almost unnoticed.

The encouraging part is that sleep can be rebuilt. A regular bedtime, a dark and cool room, and the decision to leave devices aside an hour before sleep can restore the rhythm that pressure had broken. Daylight in the morning also tells the body when to wake, so opening the curtains at once can sharpen the whole day. Quiet hours without news or noise let the mind settle, which makes falling asleep easier. Although no single trick works for everyone, small habits repeated each night help far more than one long lie-in on Sunday.

In the end, rest is not a prize we earn after the work is done; rather, it is the ground on which clear thought is built. Only those who sleep well can keep both focus and calm through a busy day, because a tired mind decides worse than a rested one.

中文译文

现代生活已悄然缩短了我们的夜晚。调查显示，许多成年人如今睡眠不足七小时，而且相当多的人在傍晚之前就感到困倦。这一变化并非偶然——它源于更亮的屏幕、更长的工作时间，以及黑暗中刷手机的安静习惯。青少年身体仍在发育、需要更多休息，可学校开学早，让他们总是睡不够。

失去的睡眠究竟让我们付出了什么？科学家解释，大脑在我们休息时会清除代谢废物，这正是睡眠差会模糊记忆、拖慢思维的原因。当人们连续几周缺觉，情绪会走低，身体抵抗疾病的能力也会下降。熬夜学习的孩子面临同样的问题，而他们的成绩往往在无人察觉原因之前就已下滑。受影响的不仅是大脑——心脏也因缺乏恢复而吃不消，体重增加的风险在不知不觉中升高。

令人鼓舞的是，睡眠可以被重建。固定的就寝时间、黑暗凉爽的房间，以及睡前一小时放下电子设备的决定，都能修复被压力打乱的节律。晨光还会告诉身体何时醒来，所以一醒来就拉开窗帘，能让一整天都更清醒。尽管没有哪种诀窍对人人有效，但每夜重复的小习惯，远比周日一次睡到中午有用得多。

归根结底，休息不是工作做完后的奖赏，而是清晰思考赖以建立的基石。只有睡眠充足的人，才能在忙碌的一天里同时保持专注与平静，因为疲惫的大脑比休息好的大脑更容易做出糟糕的决定。
